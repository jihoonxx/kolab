Human tissue,
engineered on a chip.

what we study

Vascularized & organ models

Engineered, perfusable microvascular networks inside organ and tumor models.

Tumor & immune microenvironment

Patient-derived tumors, immune cell trafficking and immune–vascular crosstalk.

High-throughput platforms

Standardized, automation-ready organ chips for drug screening and safety.

AI bioimage analysis

Virtual staining, segmentation and label-free phenotyping of 3D tissues.

    Principal Investigator

    Jihoon Ko, Ph.D. 고지훈

    Assistant Professor · Department of Bioengineering, Hanyang University kchip [at] hanyang.ac.kr · ORCID 0000-0002-6555-3728

    His research focuses on human-relevant microphysiological systems (MPS) that integrate microengineering, vascular biology, organoids, immune systems, and AI-enabled bioimage analysis. His group builds engineered human tissue models of complex physiological and pathological microenvironments, with particular interest in vascularized organ models, tumor–immune microenvironments, high-throughput microfluidic platforms, and patient-specific disease models.

            Join us

            Build human tissue on a chip with us

            We are looking for students who want to connect biology, engineering and AI. 바이오와 AI를 함께 다루며 차세대 인체 모사 플랫폼을 만들 연구원을 찾습니다.

            Who we are looking for

            • Integrated M.S.–Ph.D. (석박통합과정) applicants are preferred. M.S. applicants are also welcome.
              석박통합과정 지원자를 우대합니다. 석사과정 지원도 환영합니다.
            • Preferred: AI × Bio. Interest or experience in AI/deep learning together with biology, such as bioimage analysis, organoids or cell-culture chips.
              우대 역량: AI 바이오. 딥러닝 기반 이미지 분석, 오가노이드, 세포배양칩 개발에 관심 있는 분.

            What you will work with

            • Microfluidic chip design & 3D printing
            • 3D cell culture & organoids
            • Engineered microvasculature
            • Confocal imaging
            • Deep-learning image analysis
            • Drug screening assays
            • Industry & hospital collaborations

            How to join

            1. Send your CV and transcriptEmail both to kchip [at] hanyang.ac.kr. 이메일로 이력서(CV)와 성적증명서(사본 가능)를 보내주세요.
            2. InterviewWe will contact you for a meeting with Prof. Ko. 서류 검토 후 면담을 진행합니다.
            3. Six-month internshipBefore admission, you join the lab as an intern for about six months to get to know the research and the team. 입학 전 약 6개월간 인턴 연구원으로 함께 연구합니다.
            4. AdmissionYou begin your graduate program in the following semester. Funding and stipends are discussed individually. 인건비 등 지원 사항은 면담 후 개별적으로 논의합니다.
